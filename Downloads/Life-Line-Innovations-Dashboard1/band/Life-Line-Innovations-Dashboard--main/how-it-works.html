THE TECHNOLOGY

Three steps to
saving lives.

Life Band operates on a simple but powerful pipeline: Detect, Evaluate, and Act — all in under 3 seconds.

1

Detect

Life Band continuously reads biometric signals using clinical-grade sensors. Heart rate variability, galvanic skin response, blood pressure, and temperature are monitored 24/7.

Heart Rate Blood Pressure Stress Level SpO₂ Temperature
Continuous biometric scanning
2

Threshold Analysis

Our proprietary algorithms compare real-time readings against personalized baselines. When stress or vitals exceed safe thresholds, the system triggers an immediate evaluation.

Your baseline SAFE
Threshold exceeded ALERT
AI-powered threshold analysis
3

Alert & Intervene

When a threshold is breached, Life Band simultaneously:

  • Alerts the user with real-time feedback and breathing exercises
  • Notifies emergency contacts and designated HR personnel
  • Logs the incident for medical records and compliance reporting
  • Recommends nearby medical professionals for follow-up
Automatic multi-channel alerting
